# Finance and market analysis (Qwen3.5-4B)

Analyzes financial questions and calculations, including interest, account balances, compound growth, company valuation, DCF, FCFF, and market concepts.

Source: https://huggingface.co/alinakhay/FinVector-Market-4B

Revision: `eb72ea2456e3d8eecc46dc8c88bea40a47224047`. Check the source model card for license, training data, limitations, and usage terms before redistribution or use. This package contains metadata only; weights are fetched on demand. It was migrated from the archived Qwen3.5-4B registry in `versions/legacy-4b`. No independent quality claim is made.

## Examples

Use for: What is the monthly payment on a 1,000,000 loan for 5 years at 12% per year?

Not for: Predict tomorrow's stock price.

Output language follows the user; the source training language does not override it.
