# Medical question reasoning (Qwen3.5-4B)

Reasons about medical and biomedical questions, including exam-style differential diagnosis and treatment knowledge; not a substitute for a clinician.

Source: https://huggingface.co/naazimsnh02/Shifa-4B-SFT-LoRA

Revision: `7140e28274d935766eb265a2d0bac046f74ec742`. Check the source model card for license, training data, limitations, and usage terms before redistribution or use. This package contains metadata only; weights are fetched on demand. It was migrated from the archived Qwen3.5-4B registry in `versions/legacy-4b`. No independent quality claim is made.

## Examples

Use for: What are typical symptoms of influenza and when should one see a doctor?

Not for: Give a personal diagnosis from a photo.

Output language follows the user; the source training language does not override it.
